Movement Profile — line-movement shape vs outcomes · n={{ movement_n_total }} classified

Pre-game moneyline movement on the model-pick side, classified from odds_snapshot history (capture started 2026-06-23; purging disabled). Each shape is split for (line moved toward our pick) vs against (moved toward the opponent) — "Late Sharp against" and "Late Sharp for" are different signals and were previously lumped together, which hid the distinction that actually matters.
Early sharp most of the move happens early, then flat  |  Late sharp flat early, moves near close  |  Sustained steady drift all window  |  Reversal moves one way then retraces (direction = net result)  |  Minimal only 2 snapshots — too few to tell shape, direction still known
Flat 3+ snapshots, net move under 0.5pp — not direction-split, the move is too small for direction to mean anything  |  Static only 1 pre-game snapshot ever recorded — the line genuinely never moved  |  No data zero pre-game snapshots recorded
Each bucket needs n≥5 with stored ROI before it counts for anything beyond the 0.5 neutral prior — currently {{ movement_weights.n }} games qualify. Splitting direction roughly halves an already-thin sample per bucket — expect more 0.5-neutral buckets for a while.
